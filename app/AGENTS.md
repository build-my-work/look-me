# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

Build app UI in `src/`. Keep `.openai/hosting.json`, `worker/index.js`, `scripts/prepare-sites-build.mjs`, and `tests/sites-worker.test.mjs` intact so the same local prototype can be handed to Sites. Before a Sites handoff, run `npm run build` and `npm run test:sites`; the build must leave `dist/client/index.html`, `dist/server/index.js`, and `dist/.openai/hosting.json`.

## Lumi product decisions

- The public product and heterochromia-cat name is Lumi. Do not expose the previous “Look Me” name in user-visible Chinese or English copy, packaged product names, the website, or promotion assets. Retain existing `LookMe` / `look-me` technical identifiers, Pet/cat identifiers, asset filenames, storage keys, bundle identifiers, and behavior logic internally unless a migration is explicitly requested.
- The selected visual target is a round, realistic 3D heterochromia white cat: viewer-left eye amber gold, viewer-right eye icy blue. It is the native companion's visual identity while the ambient horizon portal remains unchanged.
- The product is a local-first desktop wellness coach, not a diagnostic medical device. Raw camera frames are processed ephemerally and never uploaded or saved.
- The always-on-top native mode stays compact and transparent. Browser preview mode supplies a defocused workspace only to make the overlay inspectable.
- Reminders are silent and non-punitive by default. Do not add streaks, scores, medical claims, or a dashboard.
- Blink detection uses MediaPipe Face Landmarker locally. Low-confidence or unavailable sensing must pause blink coaching rather than showing an unverifiable timer-only prompt.
- Blink statistics show only the rolling one-minute estimate in the compact readout. The statistics panel also derives today's effective-screen duration, current continuous seated duration, today's total seated duration, and today's stand-up count from Timeline events; do not restore current-segment average or session-total counters.
- Timeline V2 persists exactly six event types: `blink.detected`, `seated.started`, `seated.ended`, `screen.started`, `screen.ended`, and `yawn.detected`. Store exact event timestamps and collection-session coverage, never raw camera frames or aggregate values. Do not read or migrate legacy Timeline, blink, or posture stores.
- The user explicitly expects the native companion window to be draggable. The cat is the bounded drag handle; buttons remain clickable and transparent areas keep desktop click-through behavior.
- Native drag movement must keep the complete pet handle inside the active display's work area so the frameless window always has a visible recovery surface. Clamp compact movement from the measured Pet handle rather than the transparent BrowserWindow; an expanded settings/history panel may temporarily adjust the window to remain fully visible, but closing it restores the pre-expansion Pet screen anchor.
- Each detector-confirmed complete blink should replay one short blink on the cat. Non-detector states must not fabricate a mirrored blink.
- While the cat is idle and no panel is open, it may occasionally cycle through yawning, clapping, sitting down and standing back up, and spinning. Use transparent full-body sprite layers inside the existing pet shell so pet sizing, the static page background, and drag geometry stay unchanged; suppress idle actions during coaching, attention-rail motion, and reduced-motion mode. Full-body poses must play as single-frame sprite sequences with fixed scale and ground alignment—never opacity-crossfade two bodies. One clapping action raises the paws, makes six distinct contacts with a separation between each, then lowers them; sitting traverses standing and seated poses in both directions; spinning cycles through successive viewpoints instead of rotating a flat sprite.
- User-yawn mirroring uses the existing local `jawOpen` blendshape only. Keep mouth opening and closing as runtime detector state for direct Pet mirroring, but persist only the resulting `yawn.detected` point event. Accept a moderate camera score from `0.40`, treat a clearly open mouth held for about 0.9 seconds as a yawn, require a close below `0.24` before rearming, and apply an 8-second cooldown. Do not require eye signals in this minimal version, and do not change the selected idle-action preference when mirroring the user's mouth.
- The settings panel owns one locally persisted idle-action preference: automatic rotation, yawn, clap, sit, spin, or quiet. Keep this a single choice rather than an action playlist; automatic rotation is the multi-action option. Fixed choices still play occasionally on the 40-second idle timeline instead of looping continuously, and selecting an option previews it while settings remain open.
- Pet sizing is pet-only: expose small, standard, and large presets in the tray at `41% / 100% / 110%`, remember the selection locally, and keep cards and controls at their existing size. The pet image, eyelid, renderer drag region, and native edge-clamp geometry must scale together. Keep the unscaled idle panel about 10 px from the pet artwork's visible alpha edge in every preset and on either side of the attention rail; do not position it from the transparent image box.
- Distant-face blink recovery must preserve two-signal confidence: weak bilateral blendshape peaks may count only when a strong relative EAR drop confirms closure. Do not globally lower the normal blink threshold or remove wink and duration guards.
- The tray's persistent “显示小组件” setting controls the complete read-oriented capsule: current blink status, the scheduled-lock countdown, and the statistics entry. Hiding it must hide the whole capsule without stopping camera sensing, local history aggregation, or the scheduled-lock timer itself; it must not expose configuration or a manual distance-break action. A scheduled lock remains pending until the main process confirms an actual system lock; a failed or unconfirmed attempt retries after 60 seconds instead of leaving the countdown at `0:00`.
- Showing or hiding the persistent panel must keep the Pet at the same screen coordinates. Choose the panel direction from the Pet's actual display half rather than its attention-rail state: a right-side Pet anchors at the panel's right edge so the panel opens left, and a left-side Pet opens the panel right. Re-evaluate this direction after a cross-screen Pet drag while the panel is visible.
- The user approved the whole-display right-edge attention rail on 2026-08-12. Run it only while the camera is ready, a face is confidently visible, the coach is idle, and no stats/history panel is open; losing face confidence resets escalation, while manual panels and dragging pause it.
- Rail timing is fixed: hidden for the first 10 seconds after a confirmed blink, descend from the current display's upper-right during seconds 10–25, cry at the lower-right during seconds 25–30, then repeat two 5.5-second Y-axis flight rounds followed by a 2-second crying rest. Any detector-confirmed blink stops tears, mirrors the blink, recovers upward, and starts a 25-second quiet cooldown.
- Respect Reduce Motion by replacing full-display rail flight with one static crying pose near the lower-right. The generated tear PNG is a real raster asset and must remain visibly legible in the 41% small-pet preset.
- Blink coaching has no manual completion control or timer fallback. Require two detector-confirmed blinks. When a face cannot be reliably detected, dismiss any active blink prompt, show “暂未检测到人脸，眨眼提醒已暂停” in monitoring status, and restart the 25-second no-blink interval from zero after sensing returns. The 90-second cooldown applies after a prompt has occurred, not to the first prompt after monitoring starts or resumes.
- Keep the blink coaching card content-sized instead of stretching across the native window. Preserve the two existing capsule completion markers and their active state; their combined width aligns with the “陪我慢慢眨 2 下” headline. Solve excess whitespace through card sizing and spacing rather than replacing the progress UI.
- The user removed both the manual 25-minute pause and “现在远眺” actions on 2026-08-13. Distance breaks are automatic only, retain their 20-second duration and skip action, and must not have a manual trigger in the app or native menu.
- “始终显示 Lumi” is a persistent tray setting and defaults off. When enabled, hidden and post-blink cooldown phases become a visible resting pose at the current display's upper-right; descent, tears, rampage, recovery, and manual panels keep their existing behavior. The one-shot display action and its temporary 15-second reveal were removed on 2026-08-13; tray access already covers all settings.
- The pet drag region must share the pet shell's bottom transform origin in every size and rail state. Native drag QA must start inside the visible pet/handle overlap and include the 41% preset on a secondary display.
- The right edge is only the default automatic attention rail. After a user drags the pet in rail mode, preserve the dropped X coordinate and run later descent, crying, rampage, and recovery vertically at that user-selected anchor; never snap it back to the display edge on pointer release.
- “有效看屏时长” is a conservative face-visible proxy, not gaze tracking. Record `screen.started` and `screen.ended` while local camera sensing is ready, a face is confidently visible, and the coach is not in a distance break; do not gate this on the Lumi renderer page visibility because the user may be looking at other apps while the companion page is hidden. Sum interval intersections on demand. The automatic distance reminder keeps its own runtime accumulation of the same screen-observing signal and must not persist reminder lifecycle events.
- Daily history has two switchable views that share the selected date but keep independent time windows. The default event view plots every one of the six Timeline events as an exact-time point on its own categorical lane in a fixed five-minute window with five-second navigation steps and no zoom. The count view shows exactly one selected series at a time—blink, yawn, stand-up, or sit-down—and automatically chooses a local natural bucket from 1/2/5/10/15/30 minutes or 1/2/3/6/12/24 hours as its independently zoomed window changes. A covered bucket without events is zero; a bucket without collection coverage is blank, and partial edge buckets are clipped to the visible range. `screen.started` and `screen.ended` remain event-only. Today's views follow new events until the user pans or zooms the relevant view. Opening history expands the Electron window while preserving the Pet anchor, and closing it restores the compact window.
- Timeline is a minimal fact log. Do not persist reminder due, shown, completed, skipped, dismissed, user-setting, or Pet-response events; reminder decisions and UI actions remain runtime state.
- Right-clicking the visible Pet body opens the same native settings menu used by the macOS tray. Left-click never opens settings; holding and moving the left button keeps Pet dragging on the same hit region, with a 6 px movement threshold before native movement begins.
- Changing Pet size must preserve the current native window coordinates, including a user-dropped multi-display position. If a setting changes while the Pet-opened native menu is active, defer rebuilding the shared menu object until that popup closes so the next Pet click can open it again.
- Showing the existing native window for distance breaks must preserve its current coordinates. Opening settings expands the native window from `760 × 300` to `900 × 400`; opening history expands it toward `1120 × 680`, bounded by the active display. Closing either restores the compact size. Resize around the visible Pet anchor so the Pet stays at the same screen coordinates unless keeping the full panel inside the active display requires a bounded adjustment. Never reuse generic show actions to recenter a user-dragged Pet.
- Leaving the automatic attention rail must not write native window coordinates. Camera monitoring changes, schedule boundaries, lock/sleep, face loss, ordinary statistics, and distance-break state may change presentation but must preserve the window's current position. Settings and history resize may adjust the native origin solely to preserve the Pet anchor and keep the full panel on-screen. Only initial placement, direct user dragging, active rail motion, and those bounded expanded-panel resizes may move it.
- Pet-body click and drag must work while another application owns focus. Keep the transparent window click-through outside the scaled Pet handle, but use a native cursor hit-test for that handle because forwarded renderer `mousemove` is not reliable on an inactive macOS window.
- Camera monitoring settings use immediate persistence: switches apply on selection, and a valid start/end time pair applies as soon as either time changes. Do not show a separate Save action; keep an invalid pair local to the panel with an inline error until it becomes valid.
- Keep the high-frequency “监测与提醒” master switch in the shared native tray/Pet right-click menu, not duplicated inside the settings panel. The settings panel shows runtime status plus subordinate reminder and schedule preferences; the camera remains an implementation resource rather than a user-facing capability switch.
- Camera monitoring time controls use app-styled hour/minute capsules rather than native `input[type=time]`. Preserve the mist-white/deep-teal panel language, minute precision, keyboard-accessible selects, and the central clock/interval connector.
- Camera monitoring has one persistent master switch for all active monitoring and coaching. Blink, automatic distance, and sedentary reminders each have a separate persisted subordinate switch that defaults on, including when migrating older stored settings. The default optional same-day monitoring window is 09:00–21:00; preserve any valid saved window instead of migrating it. When the master switch is off—or the same-day schedule, lock state, or sleep state disallows monitoring—release the camera and suppress all prompts, attention motion, and new observation data. Turning only blink reminders off keeps camera-backed statistics and other reminders active, dismisses active blink coaching, suppresses the attention escalation, and resets blink reminder timing so re-enabling waits a fresh 25 seconds. Turning only distance reminders off keeps other monitoring active, dismisses an active automatic distance break, and resets its baseline so re-enabling waits a fresh 20 minutes. Turning only sedentary reminders off keeps posture detection and history active; re-enabling starts a fresh seated reminder interval with no catch-up prompt. Keep settings and historical data readable. Do not add weekday, multi-window, overnight, no-face shutdown, or periodic camera probing behavior.
- The shared native tray/Pet settings menu owns the high-frequency monitoring master switch plus shell-level controls: open “Lumi 设置”, Pet size, Pet persistence, panel visibility, and quit. Subordinate blink, distance, schedule, and idle-action preferences live only in the in-app settings panel; do not add distance actions to the native menu.
- Blink, mouth, and sit/away monitoring must share one camera stream and one Face Landmarker inference. Fan the resulting face frame out to independent pure signal state machines; persist only blink, yawn, screen-boundary, and seated-boundary events through the shared Timeline path.
- Sit/away monitoring uses an automatically learned stable seated eye-line position. Count one stand-up only after a clear upward trajectory reaches the upper exit zone and the face then remains absent; sudden loss, side exit, camera suspension, and ambiguous movement are unknown. The absent interval is “away” rather than verified standing, and product copy must preserve that distinction.
- Persist seated intervals as paired `seated.started` and `seated.ended` events. A seated end carries `reason: stand_up | tracking_lost`; count stand-ups only from `reason = stand_up`, and count a sit-down only when a later `seated.started` follows that confirmed stand-up in the same collection session. Continue to avoid raw frames, calibration coordinates, generic posture-change events, and duplicate stand-up events.
- Sedentary coaching uses a separate pure reminder controller whose persisted threshold defaults to 30 minutes and accepts any integer from 1 through 600 in the settings panel; disabling remains the switch's responsibility. Threshold changes apply to the current continuously seated interval. It waits behind an active blink or distance card, clears on away/unknown/monitoring suspension, and after “知道了” stays quiet until the next seated interval; do not couple this policy back into face or posture signal processing.
